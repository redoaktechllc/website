What a $79/month care plan covers

By Red Oak · Northeast Ohio

A website isn't a "set it and forget it" thing. It needs a place to live, it needs to stay secure, and every so often something needs changing — new hours, a new service, a fresh photo. The care plan covers all of that for a flat $79 a month, so you never get a surprise bill for a small favor.

What's included every month

  • Fast, secure hosting with SSL (the padlock in the address bar).
  • Your domain and DNS managed for you — no settings to wrestle with.
  • Up to 30 minutes of small edits: hours, prices, a photo, a holiday notice.
  • Monthly uptime and broken-link checks, so problems get caught early.
  • Support by text or email, with a reply within one business day.
  • A quarterly health check with a few ideas to improve the site.

What's quoted separately

Bigger jobs aren't part of the monthly fee, so the plan stays cheap for everyone. We'll always quote these up front before doing anything:

  • New pages or a redesign
  • Logo design
  • Writing new content from scratch
  • Photography
  • Advertising

Why it's monthly

Hosting and security are ongoing costs, and small edits come up throughout the year. Rolling them into one predictable fee means your site stays online, safe, and current — and you always have a real person to text when something needs to change. You can cancel with 30 days' notice, and you keep a full copy of your site.
