Most web designers ask you to pay first and trust that it'll turn out well. We do it the other way around. Before you spend a dollar, we build a real, working preview of your website so you can see exactly what you're getting.

## It's a real site, not a mock-up

The preview isn't a flat picture or a rough sketch. It's a working web page with your business on it — your name, your services, your area — that you can open on your phone and click around. We send it as a private link that only you have.

## How it works, step by step

- **You tell us about your business.** A quick call or the form on our site is enough to start — what you do, where, and the kind of work you want more of.
- **We build the preview.** Usually within a day. We'll use what's publicly available about your business and a sensible first design.
- **You look it over.** We walk through it together, and you tell us what's right and what you'd change.
- **You decide.** Like it? A deposit and a quick sign-off start the real work. Not for you? No cost, no hard feelings.

## Why we work this way

It's simple: you shouldn't have to buy a website blind. Seeing the real thing first takes the risk out of it for you, and it keeps us honest — we have to earn the yes. The preview stays private and un-indexed until you're ready to go live.
